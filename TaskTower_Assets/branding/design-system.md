# TaskTower design system

TaskTower is warm, capable and gently competitive. Use cream surfaces, navy copy and purple/pink emphasis.
Green celebrates completion; yellow signals approaching work; overdue red is always paired with a soft tint.

## Shape and depth

Cards use 20–28 px radii, buttons 14–16 px and pills 999 px. Use one soft shadow per elevation.
Illustration outlines use translucent navy rather than hard black.

## Mobile layout

Design at 390×844 first. Keep important illustration content inside the central 72% and maintain 24 px safe padding.
