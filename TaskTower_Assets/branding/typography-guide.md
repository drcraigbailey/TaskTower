# Typography

## Recommended pairing

- **Heading:** Fredoka 600–700. Rounded, confident and friendly without feeling childish.
- **Body/UI:** Nunito 400–800. Highly legible on compact Android screens.
- Both are free under the SIL Open Font License.

| Role | Family | Size | Weight | Line height |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Display | Fredoka | 40 | 700 | 1.05 |
| Page title | Fredoka | 24 | 650 | 1.15 |
| Section title | Fredoka | 18 | 650 | 1.2 |
| Body | Nunito | 15 | 500 | 1.5 |
| Label | Nunito | 12 | 800 | 1.25 |
| Microcopy | Nunito | 10 | 700 | 1.35 |
